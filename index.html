Who are we?

We run the machine layer of frontier technology. Blockchain, artificial intelligence, quantum computing — the infrastructure underneath all three, in production, around the clock.

Frontier technology is only as real as the machines it runs on. Someone has to own the boring, load-bearing parts — the nodes that settle blocks, the compute that serves models, the servers that queue quantum jobs. That is our half of the work, and everything else we do grows out of it.

We’re a small, deeply technical team with a long time horizon. No hype cycles, no roadmaps written for investors. Just engineers pushing the frontier a little further out, one system at a time.
